I am a mathematician and computer scientist with a deep interest in building rigorous theoretical foundations for artificial intelligence. Drawing from my dual background, my primary ambition for my Ph.D. is to explore the intersection of **Category Theory**, **Singular Learning Theory**, and **Safe AI**. I have spent time building a strong foundational understanding of these fields and am eager to transition into active research to help develop formal, mathematical guarantees for AI systems. Additionally, I maintain secondary exploratory interests in **Scientific Machine Learning (SciML)** and **Quantum Machine Learning (QML)**.

##### **Academic Background**
I hold a dual M.Sc. in **Mathematics** and B.E. in **Computer Science** from BITS Pilani (2024). I am currently expanding my technical expertise through the MS CS program at **Georgia Tech**, specializing in **Machine Learning**, with an expected graduation in December 2026. 

##### **Research & Academic Vision**
I am actively seeking a Ph.D. program that champions interdisciplinary exploration. Because the problems I want to tackle require bridging abstract mathematics with computer science, I am looking for academic environments that support cross-departmental collaboration.
