- In this project, we implement topology optimization with linearized buckling criteria in **Julia** using the educational paper - **Topology optimization with linearized buckling criteria in 250 lines of Matlab (Authors- Federico Ferrari, Ole Sigmund, James K. Guest)**.
- [`Here`](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-021-02854-x) is the link to paper. 
- Also, we **compare** the **efficiency** of the **Julia** implementation with corresponding **MATLAB** code.
